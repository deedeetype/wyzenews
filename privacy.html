🔒 Privacy Policy

WyzeNews by Labwyze Inc.

Effective Date: March 12, 2026
Last Updated: March 12, 2026

At WyzeNews, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you subscribe to our daily digest newsletter.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you subscribe to WyzeNews, we collect:

  • Email Address: Required for newsletter delivery
  • Subscription Date: Timestamp of when you subscribed
  • Subscription Source: Where you signed up (e.g., landing page)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We may collect:

  • IP Address: For security and fraud prevention
  • Browser Type: To optimize email rendering
  • Email Open/Click Data: To measure engagement (future feature)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Send you the WyzeNews daily digest newsletter
  • Send you a welcome email upon subscription
  • Process your unsubscribe requests
  • Improve our service and content
  • Comply with legal obligations

We will NEVER:

  • Sell your email address to third parties
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Send you spam or unsolicited emails
  • Use your data for purposes other than newsletter delivery

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Supabase (a PostgreSQL database with industry-standard encryption).

Security measures include:

  • Encrypted database connections (SSL/TLS)
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies
  • Secure API authentication
  • Regular security audits

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database hosting (see Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Netlify: Website and function hosting (see Netlify Privacy Policy)
  • Gmail: Email delivery via SMTP
  • Google AI (Gemini): Content generation (no personal data shared)
  • Perplexity AI: News aggregation (no personal data shared)

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Update your email address
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your data
  • Unsubscribe: Stop receiving emails at any time
  • Data Portability: Export your data in a readable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at: david.laborieux@gmail.com

6. Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any WyzeNews email. Unsubscribing will:

  • Mark your subscription as inactive
  • Stop all future emails immediately
  • Retain your data for 90 days (in case you want to re-subscribe)
  • Permanently delete your data after 90 days (upon request)

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for:

  • Active Subscribers: As long as you remain subscribed
  • Unsubscribed Users: 90 days (for re-subscription convenience)
  • Permanent Deletion: Upon explicit request or after 90 days of inactivity

8. Cookies

Our website does NOT use cookies for tracking or advertising. We may use minimal session cookies for form submission functionality only.

9. Children's Privacy

WyzeNews is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of WyzeNews after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Union, we comply with GDPR requirements:

  • Lawful basis: Consent (you opted in to subscribe)
  • Data minimization: We only collect essential information
  • Purpose limitation: Data used only for newsletter delivery
  • Right to erasure: You can request deletion at any time
