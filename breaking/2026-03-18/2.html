Apple Enters Foldable Phone Market

Apple’s reported move into foldable phones is a major strategic shift for the iPhone maker, which has largely stayed out of a category led by Samsung and other Android manufacturers. The decision matters because Apple’s entry could validate the segment for mainstream premium buyers and intensify competition in high-end smartphones. Initial designs are said to resemble current foldable devices already on the market, suggesting Apple may prioritize durability, usability, and supply-chain scale before introducing more ambitious hardware changes. More advanced edge-to-edge improvements could arrive as early as 2027, pointing to a phased product roadmap rather than a one-off launch. What to watch next is whether Apple can solve familiar foldable challenges such as screen creasing, hinge reliability, battery life, and app optimization. If it does, Apple could reshape the premium device landscape, pressure rivals on design, and create a new upgrade cycle for consumers.

🔑 Key Takeaways

  • Apple is reportedly preparing to enter the foldable smartphone market, a category it has so far avoided.
  • Early models are expected to look similar to existing foldables, with more advanced edge-to-edge designs potentially coming by 2027.
  • Apple’s arrival could legitimize foldables for mainstream premium buyers and increase pressure on Samsung and other rivals.
